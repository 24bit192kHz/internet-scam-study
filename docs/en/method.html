Method & sources

What is measured

Fiber: for each country, the home plan nearest 200 Mbps at the 3 biggest operators (4 where a fourth major exists), at the published monthly price. Mobile: a prepaid bundle near 10 GB / 30 days at the 3 biggest networks, plus whether an unlimited plan exists, its published cap, and 5G. The country figure is the plain average of its operators.

Income and currencies

Income: GNI per capita (Atlas method) from the World Bank, latest year available for each country. Monthly income = yearly ÷ 12. Local prices are converted to dollars with one exchange-rate snapshot for every row (data/fx.csv); exceptions are stated in the row notes.

Estimated or fallback income: North Korea, South Sudan, Eritrea, Liechtenstein, Monaco, Vatican City

Income older than 2023: Yemen (2018), Syria (2022), Cuba (2019)
