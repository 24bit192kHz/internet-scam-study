Method & sources

What is measured

Fiber: for each country, the home plan nearest 200 Mbps at the 3 biggest operators (4 where a fourth major exists), at the published monthly price. Mobile: what it costs to get at least 10 GB for a month on prepaid at the 3 biggest networks (a bigger bundle counts at full price, a smaller one is multiplied up to 10 GB when that needs at most 2×), plus whether an unlimited plan exists, its published cap, and 5G. The country figure is the plain average of its operators. Peers: countries with income per person within 1.5× (widened until there are at least 8).

Income and currencies

Income: GNI per capita (Atlas method) from the World Bank, latest year available for each country. Monthly income = yearly ÷ 12. All prices are converted to dollars with one exchange-rate snapshot of 18 November 2026 (data/fx.csv). Currencies with no single meaningful rate (YER, SYP, KPW, SSP, SDG, CUP) are not converted.

Estimated or fallback income: North Korea, South Sudan, Eritrea, Liechtenstein, Monaco, Vatican City

Income older than 2023: Yemen (2018), Syria (2022), Cuba (2019)

Audit

Every price on this site is confirmed: it appeared in text actually fetched from the operator’s (or source’s) own site in November 2026, including pages that only show prices via JavaScript (read with a headless browser). Any price we could not confirm is removed from averages, charts and tables; a country with no confirmed price is not shown. A few prices are published excluding VAT; their notes say so. Outcome of the check over all operator rows:

  • not re-confirmed: 591
  • corrected: 218
  • verified: 103
  • no source, not averaged: 91
  • added: 20
  • not offered: 11
