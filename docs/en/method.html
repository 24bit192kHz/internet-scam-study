Method & sources

What is measured

Fiber: for each country, the home plan nearest 200 Mbps at the 3 biggest operators (4 where a fourth major exists), at the published monthly price. Mobile: what it costs to get at least 10 GB for a month on prepaid at the 3 biggest networks (a bigger bundle counts at full price, a smaller one is multiplied up to 10 GB when that needs at most 2×), plus whether an unlimited plan exists, its published cap, and 5G. The country figure is the plain average of its operators. Peers: countries with income per person within 1.5× (widened until there are at least 8).

Income and currencies

Income: GNI per capita (Atlas method) from the World Bank, latest year available for each country. Monthly income = yearly ÷ 12. Re-checked rows are converted to dollars with one exchange-rate snapshot of 18 November 2026 (data/fx.csv); rows that could not be re-confirmed keep the September 2026 rate stated in their notes. Currencies with no single meaningful rate (YER, SYP, KPW, SSP, SDG, CUP) are not converted.

Estimated or fallback income: North Korea, South Sudan, Eritrea, Liechtenstein, Monaco, Vatican City

Income older than 2023: Yemen (2018), Syria (2022), Cuba (2019)

Audit

Every operator row was re-checked against the published tariff page. A new price is accepted only if it appears in text actually fetched from the source site itself. Rows with no source, or only Wikipedia, stay in the files but never enter an average. A few prices are published excluding VAT; their notes say so. Row status:

  • not re-confirmed: 698
  • corrected: 148
  • verified: 95
  • no source, not averaged: 62
  • not offered: 11
  • added: 7
